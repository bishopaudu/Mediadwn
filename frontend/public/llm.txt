# mediadwn

> A free, clean web interface for downloading videos from YouTube, TikTok, Twitter, Vimeo and 1000+ other sites. No ads, no account required.

## What it does

- Download videos as MP4 (360p, 720p, 1080p) or extract audio as MP3
- Supports playlists — select individual videos or batch download entire playlists
- Trim videos to exact start and end timestamps before downloading
- Embed subtitles directly into MP4 files
- Generate shareable download links valid for 24 hours
- Works on YouTube, Twitter/X, TikTok, Vimeo, SoundCloud and 1000+ sites

## Who it is for

- People in areas with slow or unreliable internet who need videos offline
- Anyone who wants to save videos for travel, presentations or offline viewing
- Content creators who need to archive videos
- People who want MP3 audio extracted from YouTube videos or podcasts

## How to use it

1. Go to mediadwn.xyz
2. Paste any video or playlist URL
3. Click Analyze
4. Choose format (MP4 or MP3) and quality
5. Click Download

## Technical details

- Backend: Rust (Axum framework)
- Frontend: React + Vite + Tailwind CSS
- Downloader: yt-dlp + ffmpeg
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Hosted on Railway (backend) and Render (frontend)
