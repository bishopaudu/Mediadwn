# mediadwn — Full Documentation

## Overview

mediadwn (mediadwn.xyz) is a free web-based video downloader 
built on top of yt-dlp. It provides a clean, ad-free interface 
for downloading videos from over 1000 websites including YouTube, 
Twitter/X, TikTok, Vimeo, SoundCloud and Dailymotion.

No account is required. No ads are shown. The service is 
completely free to use.

## Features

### Download formats
- MP4 video at 360p, 720p or 1080p quality
- MP3 audio extraction from any video

### Playlist support
- Detect YouTube playlists automatically
- Select individual videos from a playlist
- Batch download multiple videos at once

### Video options
- Trim video to specific start and end timestamps
- Embed subtitles into MP4 files
- Custom filename before downloading
- Speed limit control for slower connections

### Sharing
- Generate shareable download links
- Links expire after 24 hours
- No account needed to access shared links

## Supported sites

mediadwn supports all sites that yt-dlp supports including:
YouTube, YouTube Shorts, YouTube Music, Twitter/X, TikTok,
Vimeo, SoundCloud, Dailymotion, Twitch clips, Reddit videos,
BBC iPlayer, and 1000+ more.

Sites not supported: Netflix, Spotify, Apple Music, Disney+ 
and other DRM-protected streaming services.

## Privacy

- No user accounts or registration required
- No personal data collected
- Downloads are deleted from the server after 24 hours
- A random anonymous user ID is stored in the browser 
  for session continuity only
